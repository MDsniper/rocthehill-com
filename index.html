What It Is

Move. Connect. Rise.

The ROC THE HILL Challange is a free monthly outdoor fitness event designed to build community, celebrate movement, and inspire a healthier Rochester. Whether you're just starting your wellness journey or maintaining a healthy lifestyle — this event is for you.

Hosted by Dr. Sunki of E.W.A. – Everything Wellness & Aesthetics and Derrick Burgess of DB Healthx, each session combines a guided hill challenge with music, community energy, and a reward system that keeps you coming back.

  • Guided hill walk/run — up and down Cobbs Hill
  • Live DJ keeping energy high the whole time
  • Earn a commemorative badge or token on completion
  • Unlock access to exclusive wellness resources
  • Medically supervised — safe for all fitness levels
  • Free to attend — just register below

Day Of

Event Agenda

Here's your blueprint for a great Saturday morning. Arrive hydrated, wear comfortable shoes, and bring your energy.

10:00 AM
Welcome & Warm-Up
Brief welcome from Dr. Sunki and Derrick Burgess, followed by a 5-minute dynamic warm-up to prep your body — leg swings, hip circles, light cardio activation. Know before you go: safety tips, trail etiquette, and pacing guidance.
10:10 AM
The Challenge Begins
All participants take on the hill — walk, jog, or run at your own pace. Complete one full up-and-back to earn your badge. Multiple laps encouraged. DJ keeps the music pumping. EWA team and Derrick are on course for motivation and support.
11:00 AM
Cool Down & Stretch
Group cool-down led by Derrick — static stretching, breathing exercises, and recovery tips tailored for weight loss and general wellness participants.
11:15 AM
Badge & Token Ceremony
Complete finishers receive their monthly badge or challenge token. Unlock access to exclusive EWA wellness resources. Community photos, celebrations, and good vibes.
11:30 AM
Community Connect & Close
Hang out, connect with fellow participants, and chat with the EWA team about medically supervised wellness options. Learn about upcoming events and next month's challenge. Wrap by noon.
